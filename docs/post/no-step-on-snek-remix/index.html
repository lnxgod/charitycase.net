A Dad’s Remix of a Family AI Experiment

Inspired by the creativity of my kids, I took the same idea behind NO STEP ON SNEK and added my own twist. With a few updates, tweaks, and personal touches, this remix shows how quickly a working concept can be reimagined and reinvented. My hope is to demonstrate the ease and fun of turning an idea into something new—even in just one evening.

Building on Retro Nostalgia

The remix keeps the heart of the original: an 8-bit-inspired adventure packed with challenges and retro charm. Whether you’re a fan of classic arcade action or just love quirky games, the essence of NO STEP ON SNEK is still here—only this time, reimagined with fresh ideas and a few gameplay surprises.

From Curious Minds to Collaborative Creativity

As the dad in this story, I’m not a professional coder, but working with AI made it possible to create something fun and functional in record time. Just like my kids did, I used OpenAI’s ChatGPT to describe my updates, fix errors, and fine-tune the game. The result? A remix that’s uniquely mine, but also a testament to how accessible creativity can be when powered by AI tools.

Thank You for Joining Our Journey

This remix is part of our family’s ongoing experiment to explore what’s possible with AI, creativity, and collaboration. Whether you’re here for the retro vibes or curious about AI-driven game design, we hope you enjoy NO STEP ON SNEK: The Remix.

Stay tuned—there are always more exciting and experimental projects in the works. Feedback is welcome!
