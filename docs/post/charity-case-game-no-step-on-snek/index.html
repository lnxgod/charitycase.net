Charity Case GAME! NO STEP ON SNEK!!!!

Will HatzerJan 12, 20252 min read

Play the mobile version or open the desktop version.

A Nostalgic Nod to 8-Bit Classics

Introducing NO STEP ON SNEK, a brand-new game inspired by classic 8-bit arcade adventures. If you grew up smashing buttons on old-school consoles, you’ll love the retro flavor woven into this experience. But here’s the real kicker: not a single line of code was manually written to create it.

Created by Kids with 0 Coding Experience

The development team behind NO STEP ON SNEK consists of two young siblings—ages 7 and 15—who took on the challenge of game creation without traditional programming. Their older sibling, who does have coding experience, stepped aside as a play tester, providing gameplay feedback but not writing the code. Meanwhile, their dad’s role was to help describe their requests to OpenAI and run the game code for them.

AI at the Helm

The family relied entirely on OpenAI’s ChatGPT 01 PRO model to transform their ideas into reality. Over the course of just four hours, they described gameplay elements, requested custom features, and used ChatGPT to fix errors and polish the code, all without manually typing code. Any time they ran into a glitch, they simply pasted the error message into ChatGPT and explained what they wanted. ChatGPT then automatically generated the necessary HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to bring the game to life.

Custom (Challenge) Mode

By default, the game launches in “Custom” mode, though it might as well be called Challenge Mode. It’s designed to be very difficult, especially if you’re aiming to reach the final stage: Level 5. Your goal? Rack up the highest score possible before the clock—or the sneks—get you.

A Family’s Experiment

This project began as a simple experiment: what can be done when curious minds, even with no coding experience, engage with powerful AI tools? The result is a delightful throwback platformer, sprinkled with imaginative touches reminiscent of favorite retro titles.

Grateful for the Support

The family behind NO STEP ON SNEK wants to extend a huge thank you to everyone who’s been following their creative journey. This nonprofit venture is only the beginning of many imaginative projects.

Ready to Play?

Now it’s your turn to dive into NO STEP ON SNEK. Tangle with sneks and get your nostalgic fix of pixel-perfect action.

NO STEP ON SNEK was fully developed with the help of OpenAI’s ChatGPT 01 PRO model—and even this blog post was written by AI. The possibilities for creativity are limitless when curious minds meet cutting-edge technology. Enjoy the game!
