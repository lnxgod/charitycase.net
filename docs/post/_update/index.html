Update!

Will HatzerAug 31, 20241 min read

DEF CON didn’t go as planned. I got COVID the first day and so did others on the team. We didn’t do as well as we had hoped, but out of 94 teams we were in the top ten with only a fraction of the team able to participate.

I am going to be kicking off the toy drive in the coming weeks. If you like Map6Bot or I’ve helped you make your car faster, you’ll have a chance to donate very soon.

We did finish the solar shed. I’ve got ten 320-watt panels, a PowMr 5K unit, and two Chins 48V 100Ah batteries. We are using it to charge my son’s Tesla and run some freezers at the house. Learn more on my YouTube channel.

Upcoming Projects

  • Golf cart: ground-effects LED kit, headlights, and taillights.
  • House: updated WLED systems with new power supplies and weatherproof boxes, plus the mega tree.
  • Motorcycle: plastidipping my bike purple to match my Corvette.

I’ll be making videos of these projects so folks can learn how to do this at home.
